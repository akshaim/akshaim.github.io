IoT Lab - Electronics 102

Wireless Communication

Wireless Communication is essentially serial communication over air.Xbee, Bluetooth , Radio and WiFi are the most common wireless solutions available in the market.



How do you choose a wireless module

  • Range
  • Power consumption
  • Data Rate
  • Propogation Model
  • Network Topology
  • Cost

Wireless Modules in IoT Lab

IoT Lab has the following wireless modules which offers a healthy start to the world of wireless . You may feel free to try out more solutions such as Xbee , Bluetooth LE etc. Click on each to know more

  • RF Module - NRF24L01
  • Wifi Module - ESP8266 (not written)
  • WiFi Dev Board - NodeMCU
  • GSM Board - SIM900A
