PreFab Training

About

Pre Fab training is an effort from Kerala Start Up Mission to prepare Makers for Fab Academy.The training is a 4 Week intense course with an additional week for developing solutions, based on what learned during the course.I was given the opportunity to learn with an young group of makers at Fab Lab Trivandrum . Pre Fab programme was mentored by Franscisco and Luciano.

What we tried

Equipments on which we were trained.

  • ShopBot CNC
  • UltiMaker 2
  • Roland Modella
  • Rolland GX-24
  • Trotec Laser
  • Molding and Casting
